## 20-question probe across operation families

I probed the pipeline with one representative question per analytical
operation family and recorded whether the response was semantically
correct. 10 cases returned a confident ANSWER whose SQL did not answer
the question. Nine of them shared a single root cause: when the planner
cannot bind the unsupported operation keyword, it falls through to a
`total_revenue` fallback and emits a semantically unrelated query.

## Shared architectural fix

A single `safety/capability_check.py` module encodes every unsupported
operation keyword. The orchestrator consults it in the pre-SQL safety
sequence (destructive → forecast → unsupported-capability → planner).

The gate is intentionally conservative:
* each unsupported construct is named by its own regex;
* refusal routes to CLARIFY with a specific user-facing reason;
* legitimate questions that only mention gate-adjacent words
  ("above average", "below the median", relative-to-stat intents) are
  explicitly excluded by the pattern design.

## Architectural layers already correct (preserved)

* AnalyticalPlan IR as shared representation
* Dialect validator (SQLite vs. Postgres/MSSQL/MySQL)
* Semantic fulfilment validator
* Fidelity validator (plan ↔ SQL)
* Decision policy ANSWER/WARN/CLARIFY/ABSTAIN
* Destructive-request pre-gate
* Forecast pre-gate
* Relative-period resolution with `INSIGHTFLOW_REF_DATE`
* Explanation safety (strip forecast phrasing)
* 211 passing tests (prior work)

## Not scoped here

* Stateful conversational follow-up resolution.
* Implementing median / percentile / rolling / top-per-group SQL
  (the fix is to refuse honestly until implemented).
* Multi-dataset join semantics on uploaded datasets (single-table by
  design).
* Dialect validation for engines other than SQLite (noop by design).
